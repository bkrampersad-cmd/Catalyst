# Catalyst

**Chemistry, accelerated.** A free, installable study companion for high school chemistry: an interactive color periodic table, step-by-step chemistry calculators, and a study guide that follows the class syllabus. It is built for NYS Regents (Enriched) Chemistry and works offline once installed.

**Live at:** https://catalyst.beeransapps.com *(not yet deployed)*

## Features

- **Periodic table.** All 118 elements, color-coded by category. You can switch the view to state at room temperature, electronegativity, ionization energy, atomic mass, or density to see periodic trends. Search works by name, symbol, or number. Tap an element for a detail card with:
  - Shell configuration in Regents style (2-8-1) and orbital notation ([Ne]3s1)
  - A Bohr diagram drawn from the element's own data
  - Valence electrons, common charges, melting and boiling points, density, and discovery year
  - A plain-English note on why the valence count matters
- **Calculators.** Every calculator shows its work, not just the answer:
  - Molar Mass & Moles: handles parentheses and hydrates, shows percent composition, and converts grams ↔ moles ↔ particles
  - Equation Balancer: exact integer math, with an atom-count check on each side
  - Gas Laws: combined gas law (P, V, T) and the ideal gas law, with unit and °C → K conversion
  - Solutions: molarity (in grams or moles), dilution, and ppm
  - pH & pOH: converts between any of pH, pOH, [H⁺] and [OH⁻]
  - Measurement: sig fig counter and rounding, percent error, and density
- **Study Guide.** The units follow the class syllabus order. Unit 1 (Matter & Measurement) is complete, with six illustrated lessons and a practice quiz that explains each answer. Progress is saved on the device.
- **Photo Tutor** *(coming later)*. Snap a problem and get hints first, then the steps, then the answer.

## Install

Open the site in Chrome (Android, Chromebook, Windows, Mac) or Safari (iPhone/iPad).

- **Chrome:** use **Install app** in the menu.
- **Safari:** tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

After that it runs like a regular app, including offline.
